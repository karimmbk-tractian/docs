SmartTrac sensors continuously monitor your machines by measuring vibration, temperature, and RPM — streaming data to TRACTIAN's AI engine around the clock. This guide walks you through physically mounting a SmartTrac sensor, pairing it to a Gateway Ultra, and verifying that live data is flowing into your asset dashboard.

---

## What you'll need

Before you begin, make sure you have the following ready:

- **SmartTrac sensor** — confirm the serial number label is intact and readable
- **Gateway Ultra** — powered on and connected to your network
- **TRACTIAN mobile app** — installed on your iOS or Android device and signed in
- **Asset created in the platform** — the machine must already exist in your asset tree before you can attach a sensor

<Note>
  If the asset doesn't exist yet, create it under **Assets > Add Asset** before continuing. A sensor cannot be paired to a machine that hasn't been registered in the platform.
</Note>

## Installing your SmartTrac sensor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mount the sensor on the machine">
    Attach the SmartTrac sensor to the machine casing as close as possible to the bearing housing. The mounting surface must be **flat, clean, and free of paint or corrosion** to ensure proper vibration transmission.

    - Use the **magnetic base** for ferrous surfaces — press firmly until fully seated
    - Use the **adhesive mounting pad** or **stud mount** for non-ferrous or painted surfaces
    - Orient the sensor so the axis label aligns with the primary measurement direction (radial or axial)

    <Warning>
      Never mount the sensor on a flexible panel, rubber-lined surface, or a location with direct exposure to cutting fluid jets. Poor mounting contact is the leading cause of inaccurate readings.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Power on and check the LED indicator">
    SmartTrac sensors are powered by a long-life industrial battery — no wiring required. After mounting, press and hold the side button for **2 seconds** to power on the device.

    Check the LED status:

    | LED behavior | Status |
    |---|---|
    | **Solid green (3 sec)** | Powered on, ready to pair |
    | **Slow blue blink** | Searching for Gateway |
    | **Fast blue blink** | Connected, transmitting data |
    | **Red blink** | Error — check battery or proximity to Gateway |

  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the TRACTIAN app and select your asset">
    In the TRACTIAN mobile app, navigate to the **Assets** tab and tap the machine you want to monitor. On the asset detail screen, tap **Add Sensor**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scan the QR code or enter the serial number">
    Point your phone camera at the QR code printed on the SmartTrac sensor label. The app will automatically detect and populate the sensor details.

    If the QR code is damaged or unreadable, tap **Enter manually** and type in the serial number printed below the barcode.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm pairing and verify data streaming">
    The app will connect the sensor to the nearest Gateway Ultra and run a pairing handshake. Once confirmed, you'll see a **"Sensor active"** status on the asset card.

    Return to the asset dashboard on the web platform. Within a few minutes, the sensor tile should show a live temperature reading and the first trend data point. Full AI-powered insights require at least **24 hours of continuous data collection**.

    <Note>
      Allow a minimum of **24 hours** before expecting your first AI-generated insights. The Auto Diagnosis™ engine needs enough samples to establish a baseline and detect anomalies reliably.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sampling modes

SmartTrac sensors support three sampling modes. You can configure or change the mode at any time from the asset's **Condition Monitoring** settings.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Auto Diagnosis™">
    **Auto Diagnosis™** is the default mode and the one most customers use for day-to-day monitoring. The AI engine automatically collects a full vibration sample every **30 minutes**, analyzes it against 35+ fault signatures, and generates insights without any manual action on your part.

    - Runs continuously in the background
    - No configuration required
    - Optimized for battery longevity (3–5 year battery life)
    - Insight alerts are generated automatically when anomalies are detected

    <Tip>
      Auto Diagnosis™ is the recommended mode for all production assets. Reserve Expert Sample for one-off deep-dive investigations on specific fault hypotheses.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Trend Sample">
    **Trend Sample** captures short-interval velocity and acceleration metrics at a higher frequency than standard Auto Diagnosis™ cycles. Use this mode when you want tighter monitoring on an asset that's showing early warning signs.

    Metrics collected per sample:
    - **RMS** (Root Mean Square) — overall energy level
    - **Peak-to-Peak** — maximum displacement range
    - **Crest Factor** — ratio of peak to RMS, useful for detecting impulsive events like bearing defects

    Trend Sample stores results as time-series data, making it easy to spot gradual degradation trends on the asset dashboard.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Expert Sample">
    **Expert Sample** gives you full control over sampling parameters for detailed spectral investigation. Use this mode when you need a high-resolution spectrum to confirm or rule out a specific failure mode.

    Configurable parameters:

    | Parameter | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Duration** | Total collection time per sample |
    | **Resolution** | Frequency resolution of the spectrum (Hz per line) |
    | **Spectrum interval** | Frequency range to capture |
    | **Number of lines** | Spectral resolution density |

    Expert Samples are triggered on demand and are not affected by the 30-minute Auto Diagnosis™ schedule. Results appear in the **Spectral Analysis** workspace immediately after collection.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sensor placement guidelines

Proper placement is critical to data quality. Follow these guidelines for every installation:

- **Mount near the bearing housing** — the closer to the load zone, the better the fault signal-to-noise ratio
- **Use a flat, rigid surface** — vibration must transfer directly from the machine structure to the sensor
- **Avoid loose or indirect mounting** — never place the sensor on a bracket, cover, or panel that isn't part of the main machine structure
- **Keep clear of heat sources** — do not mount directly above exhaust vents or steam pipes; the sensor is rated to **250°F (121°C)** but sustained heat reduces battery life
- **Separate sensors per bearing** — on motors and pumps with multiple bearings, use one sensor per bearing housing for precise fault isolation
- **Maintain Gateway proximity** — ensure the sensor is within **330 ft (100 m)** of a Gateway Ultra if obstacles are present, or up to **3,300 ft (1,000 m)** in line-of-sight environments
