Work orders are the operational backbone of TRACTIAN's CMMS — every maintenance task, whether triggered by a scheduled plan, an AI-generated insight, or an urgent breakdown, is tracked as a work order. From the moment you create a work order to the moment a technician marks it complete in the field, TRACTIAN gives you full visibility into what's being done, by whom, and on which asset.

---

## Work order types

TRACTIAN supports three types of work orders. Choose the right type to ensure accurate reporting and to keep your maintenance history meaningful.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Corrective" icon="wrench">
    Reactive maintenance triggered by an equipment failure or reported problem. Use this type when an asset has already broken down or is performing below acceptable thresholds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Preventive" icon="calendar-check">
    Scheduled maintenance carried out at regular intervals to prevent failures before they occur. Preventive work orders are typically auto-generated by a maintenance plan.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Predictive" icon="brain-circuit">
    AI-generated work orders created directly from a TRACTIAN insight. When the platform detects an anomaly or an impending failure, it recommends a corrective action — one click turns that recommendation into a work order.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Creating a work order

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From an AI Insight">
    TRACTIAN's AI continuously monitors your assets and surfaces insights when it detects anomalies. You can convert any insight into a work order without leaving the insight detail view.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the insight">
        Navigate to **Insights** in the left sidebar and select the insight you want to act on. Review the AI's analysis, recommended action, and severity level.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a work order">
        Click **Create Work Order** in the top-right corner of the insight detail panel. TRACTIAN automatically populates the asset, description, and recommended priority based on the insight data.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review and assign">
        Confirm or update the pre-filled fields — adjust the priority, assign a technician, and set a due date.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save the work order">
        Click **Save**. The work order is created with **Open** status and linked to the originating insight. The assigned technician receives a notification on their mobile app.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manually">
    Create a work order from scratch when you receive a reported failure, complete a physical inspection, or need to schedule a one-off task.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Navigate to Work Orders">
        Go to **CMMS** → **Work Orders** in the left sidebar, then click **+ New Work Order**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Fill in the work order details">
        Enter a clear title, select the affected asset, and choose the work order type (Corrective, Preventive, or Predictive). Add a description explaining the problem or the task to be performed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set priority and scheduling">
        Choose a priority level (Low, Medium, High, or Critical), set the due date, and enter the estimated hours needed to complete the task.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Assign the work order">
        Select the responsible technician or team from the **Assigned To** field. Add any parts or materials needed under **Parts Needed**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save and notify">
        Click **Save**. The work order appears in the queue with **Open** status, and the assigned technician is notified automatically.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From the mobile app">
    Technicians and supervisors can create work orders directly in the field using the TRACTIAN mobile app — useful when a problem is discovered during a routine inspection or lubrication route.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the mobile app">
        Log in to the TRACTIAN mobile app and tap **Work Orders** in the bottom navigation bar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap New Work Order">
        Tap the **+** button in the top-right corner to open the new work order form.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select the asset">
        Search for or scan the asset QR code to link the work order to the correct machine.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Fill in details">
        Enter a title, select the work order type, add a description, and set the priority. You can attach photos directly from your phone's camera.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Assign and save">
        Assign the work order to yourself or another technician, set the due date, and tap **Save**. The work order syncs to the platform immediately.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      If you're in an area without connectivity, the work order is saved locally and syncs automatically once your device reconnects to the network. No data is lost.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Work order fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Title** | A concise name for the task (e.g., "Replace bearing on Pump A-12"). |
| **Asset** | The specific machine or component the work order applies to. |
| **Priority** | Urgency level: **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, or **Critical**. |
| **Type** | Corrective, Preventive, or Predictive. |
| **Assigned To** | The technician or team responsible for completing the task. |
| **Due Date** | The date by which the work order must be completed. |
| **Estimated Hours** | Expected time to complete the task. Used for workload planning. |
| **Parts Needed** | Materials or spare parts required. Linked to inventory when available. |
| **Description** | Detailed instructions, failure symptoms, or context for the technician. |

## Work order statuses

Work orders move through a defined lifecycle from creation to closure. Understanding each status helps you manage your team's queue and spot bottlenecks quickly.

<Accordion title="Open">
  The work order has been created and is waiting to be picked up. No technician has started work yet. Use this status for all newly created work orders awaiting assignment or scheduling.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="In Progress">
  A technician has accepted the work order and has begun the maintenance task. The clock starts when the technician taps **Start** on their mobile app, allowing accurate time tracking.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Waiting for Parts">
  The technician has started the task but cannot complete it because a required part or material is unavailable. Setting this status pauses the work and flags the need for procurement, keeping the work order visible to supervisors.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Completed">
  The technician has finished the task, recorded their work notes, and submitted the work order. A completion timestamp and the technician's notes are saved to the asset's maintenance history.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cancelled">
  The work order is no longer needed — for example, if the asset was decommissioned or the issue resolved itself. Cancelled work orders are retained in the history log for reporting purposes.
</Accordion>

## Completing a work order

Technicians complete work orders directly in the TRACTIAN mobile app. Completion captures a full record of what was done, which is automatically linked to the asset's history and failure library.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assigned work order">
    On the mobile app, tap **Work Orders** and select the task from your queue. Tap **Start** to move the work order to **In Progress** and begin time tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Perform the maintenance task">
    Carry out the maintenance work as described in the work order instructions. Reference any checklists or procedural notes included by the planner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes and photos">
    Tap **Add Note** to document what you found and what you did. Tap the camera icon to attach photos — before-and-after images are especially useful for audits and future reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record time and parts used">
    Enter the actual time spent on the task and log any parts or materials consumed. This updates inventory records and contributes to accurate maintenance cost tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the work order">
    Tap **Complete** to close the work order. Your notes, photos, time, and parts are saved and immediately synced to the platform. The asset's maintenance history is updated automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Add detailed notes every time you complete a work order — even for routine tasks. Over time, this data trains TRACTIAN's AI to recognize failure patterns specific to your assets and generate more accurate predictive insights.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Offline support:** All work order actions — including creation, status updates, notes, and photo attachments — are available offline in the TRACTIAN mobile app. Changes are stored locally and sync automatically when your device reconnects. You never need to wait for a signal to complete your work.
</Note>
