## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.tractian.com](https://app.tractian.com) or contact your TRACTIAN account manager to provision your company workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your asset tree">
    Add your plant, areas, and machines to TRACTIAN's asset tree. You can import in bulk or add assets one at a time from the web app or mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install sensors">
    Mount TRACTIAN SmartTrac sensors on your machines and pair them to a Gateway. Sensors begin streaming vibration, temperature, and RPM data within minutes of installation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your first insights">
    TRACTIAN's AI analyzes incoming data and surfaces the first health insights within 24 hours. Review them in the **Monitoring** module and create work orders directly from any fault diagnosis.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Need help with hardware installation or onboarding? Contact your TRACTIAN Customer Success Manager or email support@tractian.com.
</Note>
