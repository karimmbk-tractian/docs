The TRACTIAN mobile app puts the full power of your maintenance program in your pocket. Field technicians can complete work orders, perform inspection routes, and scan QR codes to pull up any asset on the shop floor — all without returning to a desk. Maintenance supervisors can monitor asset health, review AI-generated fault diagnoses, and act on critical alerts the moment they arrive.

## Install the app

Download the TRACTIAN app for your device and sign in with your company credentials.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Download on the App Store" icon="apple" href="https://apps.apple.com">
    Install TRACTIAN on your iPhone or iPad running iOS 15 or later. Search for **TRACTIAN** in the App Store and tap **Get**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Get it on Google Play" icon="google-play" href="https://play.google.com">
    Install TRACTIAN on your Android device running Android 9 or later. Search for **TRACTIAN** in the Google Play Store and tap **Install**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

After installing, open the app and enter the email address and password associated with your TRACTIAN account. If your company uses single sign-on (SSO), tap **Sign in with SSO** and enter your organization's domain.

<Note>
  You need an active TRACTIAN account to log in. If you don't have one yet, ask your company's TRACTIAN administrator to add you as a user — they can do this from the **Users** section in the TRACTIAN web app.
</Note>

## App navigation

The bottom navigation bar gives you quick access to every major area of the app:

| Tab | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | A summary of your facility's health, open work orders assigned to you, and today's scheduled inspections. |
| **Assets** | Your full asset tree organized by site, area, and machine. Tap any asset to see its health status, sensor readings, and maintenance history. |
| **Work Orders** | All work orders assigned to you or your team. Filter by status, priority, or asset to find what you need. |
| **Inspections** | Your scheduled inspection routes and lubrication routes. Start a route, follow the guided checklist, and submit when complete. |
| **Notifications** | A log of all alerts, work order assignments, and system messages. Tap any notification to go directly to the related record. |

## Asset Copilot

Asset Copilot is TRACTIAN's AI assistant built directly into the mobile app. Tap the Copilot icon on any asset screen to ask questions in plain language — things like *"What are the most common failure modes for this pump?"* or *"How often should I grease this bearing?"* Copilot draws on the asset's history, sensor data, manufacturer documentation, and TRACTIAN's industrial knowledge base to give you specific, actionable answers. You can also ask Copilot to summarize recent fault events or suggest next maintenance steps.
