TRACTIAN is an end-to-end industrial IoT and AI maintenance platform built for manufacturers who want to move from reactive repairs to true predictive maintenance. By combining hardware sensors, machine-learning fault detection, a full CMMS, and executive dashboards in a single system, TRACTIAN gives your entire maintenance organization — from technicians on the floor to plant managers in the boardroom — a shared, real-time picture of asset health. This documentation covers everything you need to configure the platform, manage your assets, act on AI-generated insights, and measure the results.

## Key capabilities

- **AI fault detection** — Continuous analysis across 35+ failure modes including bearing wear, shaft misalignment, cavitation, imbalance, and electrical faults, with ranked severity and recommended actions.
- **SmartTrac IoT sensors** — Industrial-grade sensors rated IP69K and Class 1 Div 1, streaming vibration (up to 64 kHz), temperature, and RPM data over 4G/LTE with a 3–5 year battery life.
- **Integrated CMMS** — Work orders, preventive maintenance plans, and lubrication routes in one place, with full two-way sync to your existing ERP or CMMS via REST API integrations.
- **Offline-capable mobile app** — Native iOS and Android apps that queue actions locally and sync automatically when connectivity is restored.
- **Multi-channel notifications** — Configurable alerts delivered by push notification, email, SMS, WhatsApp, or voice call, routed by severity and role.
- **Executive Mode** — Multi-company, multi-plant dashboards that aggregate OEE, asset health scores, and maintenance KPIs for leadership visibility without needing separate reports.
