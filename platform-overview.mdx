TRACTIAN is an end-to-end industrial maintenance platform that closes the loop between raw sensor data and measurable operational outcomes. A SmartTrac sensor detects an anomaly, the AI engine diagnoses the fault, a work order is created and executed, and the result feeds back into your analytics — all within a single connected system. No data silos, no manual handoffs between tools. This page explains each module, how they are designed to work together, and the hardware and connectivity options that underpin the platform.

## The TRACTIAN platform

## How it all connects

Every value TRACTIAN delivers flows through a single, continuous loop that starts with a sensor and ends with a measurable outcome recorded in your analytics.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sensor data collection">
    A SmartTrac sensor mounted on your machine captures vibration waveforms, temperature readings, and rotational speed continuously. Data transmits over 4G/LTE through a TRACTIAN Gateway to the cloud processing pipeline in near real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI fault detection">
    The AI engine analyzes incoming signals against failure-mode models trained on millions of industrial machine readings. When it detects a pattern consistent with a known fault, it generates an **Insight** — a structured diagnosis that includes the failure mode, severity level, confidence score, and recommended corrective action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work order creation">
    From any Insight, you or the system can automatically generate a **Work Order** in the CMMS. The work order is pre-populated with the asset, fault description, and recommended task. It is assigned to a technician, prioritized based on asset criticality and fault severity, and queued in their mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintenance execution">
    The assigned technician receives a multi-channel notification (push, email, SMS, WhatsApp, or voice), opens the work order on their mobile device, follows the step-by-step instructions, and marks the job complete — even offline. Any parts used, time logged, or observations noted are captured in the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analytics and continuous improvement">
    Completed work orders feed back into your OEE dashboards, MTTR/MTBF calculations, and asset health scores. Over time, the platform surfaces patterns — recurring faults, high-cost assets, underperforming areas — so your reliability program becomes progressively more targeted and efficient.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The faster your team closes the loop from Insight to completed Work Order, the richer TRACTIAN's feedback data becomes. Closed work orders with root-cause notes and parts records directly improve the precision of future AI diagnoses for your specific machines.
</Info>

## Hardware: SmartTrac sensors

SmartTrac is TRACTIAN's purpose-built industrial IoT sensor, designed to survive demanding manufacturing environments and deliver high-fidelity data without requiring wired infrastructure.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Measurement capabilities" icon="gauge-high" href="/monitoring/sensors-setup">
    - **Vibration:** up to 64 kHz sampling rate, capturing high-frequency fault signatures that lower-resolution sensors miss
    - **Temperature:** continuous surface temperature monitoring with configurable alert thresholds
    - **RPM:** real-time rotational speed tracking for accurate frequency-domain analysis
  </Card>
  <Card title="Industrial ratings & connectivity" icon="shield-check" href="/monitoring/sensors-setup">
    - **IP69K** — sealed against high-pressure washdowns and dust ingress
    - **Class 1 Division 1** — certified for use in hazardous, potentially explosive atmospheres
    - **4G/LTE** connectivity through TRACTIAN Gateway, with local data buffering during network outages
    - **3–5 year battery life** under standard measurement intervals, with no wiring required
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  SmartTrac sensors buffer data locally if the Gateway loses connectivity. No readings are lost during temporary network interruptions — the buffer syncs automatically when the connection is restored.
</Tip>

<Accordion title="Mounting options">
  SmartTrac supports three mounting configurations to suit different machine types and installation permanence requirements:

  - **Magnetic base** — Quick-attach for temporary surveys or machines with ferrous housings. Ideal for route-based data collection and pilot deployments.
  - **Adhesive pad** — Permanent installation on non-ferrous surfaces. Rated for continuous use in environments with moderate vibration.
  - **Threaded stud (M8)** — Bolted installation for high-vibration or high-temperature applications where adhesive or magnetic mounts are insufficient. Provides the most rigid coupling and highest signal fidelity.

  Consult the SmartTrac Installation Guide or your Customer Success engineer for placement recommendations specific to your machine type (motors, pumps, fans, gearboxes, compressors, etc.).
</Accordion>
